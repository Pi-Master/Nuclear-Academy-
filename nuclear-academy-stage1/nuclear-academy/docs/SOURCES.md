# Candidate sources (NOT yet verified)

These are well-known sources proposed as references for chapters. None has been checked for edition, exact title or content in this project yet. Set `verified: true` on a reference only after confirming it. Do not cite anything here as fact until then.

- **Nuclear physics and reactor physics:** Krane, *Introductory Nuclear Physics*; Lamarsh and Baratta, *Introduction to Nuclear Engineering*; Duderstadt and Hamilton, *Nuclear Reactor Analysis*; Lewis, *Fundamentals of Nuclear Reactor Physics*; Stacey, *Nuclear Reactor Physics*.
- **Thermal hydraulics and power cycles:** Todreas and Kazimi, *Nuclear Systems*; Incropera et al., *Fundamentals of Heat and Mass Transfer*; Cengel and Boles, *Thermodynamics: An Engineering Approach*; White, *Fluid Mechanics*.
- **Radiation science:** Knoll, *Radiation Detection and Measurement*; Shultis and Faw, *Radiation Shielding*; Turner, *Atoms, Radiation, and Radiation Protection*; ICRP recommendations.
- **Materials:** Was, *Fundamentals of Radiation Materials Science*; Olander, *Fundamental Aspects of Nuclear Reactor Fuel Elements*.
- **Fusion:** Freidberg, *Plasma Physics and Fusion Energy*; Wesson, *Tokamaks*; ITER Organization public material.
- **Safety and regulation:** IAEA Safety Standards Series (including Fundamental Safety Principles, SF-1); IAEA INSAG reports; national regulators (US NRC, UK ONR).
- **Fuel cycle and waste:** IAEA Nuclear Energy Series; OECD Nuclear Energy Agency publications; World Nuclear Association information library.
- **Applications:** IAEA educational material on nuclear medicine and isotopes; Cherry, Sorenson and Phelps, *Physics in Nuclear Medicine*.
